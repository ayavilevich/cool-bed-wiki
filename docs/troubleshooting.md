## System running but no cooling happens

* Feel the pad with your hands. The water channels should swell slightly as water circulates.
* Check whether water is circulating in the containers. Avoid lifting the hose out of the water, as this can allow air into the loop.
* Make sure the pumps are connected correctly to the hoses. A typical pump will have an axial inlet and a radial outlet.
* Try to stop and start the pump a few times manually.
* If your controller has current monitoring functionality, observe the current of the pump to check its operation.
* Check the temperature of the water. If needed, add or replace ice packs.
* Operate the pumps but turning on various manual modes in the UI and see if that make a difference to water temperature or flow.

## Unusual noise during pump operation

* Make sure the pump is fully submerged and covered with water.
* Make sure the inlet port is facing up so that any trapped air can escape.
* Manually start and stop the pump and see if any bubbles leave the pump.
* Check that you are running at the maximum speed setting (unless you have a reason not to).
* Try to manipulate the pad with your hands to get any trapped air out.
* Use the "Prime" function for 15-30 minutes and check for improvement.

## Humming noise during pump operation

* Isolate your pump from the container. Use suction cups, if available, or a silicone pad.
* Isolate the container from the floor or surrounding surfaces. Try putting a rug under it.
* Place the containers farther from the bed using longer hoses.
* Reduce the system voltage to run the pumps at a lower speed.

More research is needed to achieve silent operation. Please experiment and [share your findings](index.md#contributing).
